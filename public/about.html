Spendsheet

Log expenses in seconds, on your phone or computer. Every expense is saved in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive.

Open Spendsheet

What it does

  • Add an expense in a few taps: amount, category, date and an optional note.
  • See any month's expenses day by day, with the month's total.
  • See where your money went: spending by category, and how it compares with the month before.
  • Use your own categories, your currency, and light or dark mode.
  • Each financial year (April to March, or January to December) gets its own spreadsheet, with a tab for every month.
  • Open the spreadsheet any time to edit, sort or chart your expenses yourself. Changes you make there show up in the app.

Your data stays yours

You sign in with Google. Spendsheet then creates a folder called Spendsheet in your Google Drive, and keeps your expenses, categories and settings in spreadsheets inside it.

Spendsheet has no server and no database of its own: the app runs in your browser and talks to Google directly. It can only see the files it creates, never the rest of your Drive. There are no ads and no tracking.
