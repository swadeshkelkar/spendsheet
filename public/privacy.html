Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Spendsheet is an expense tracker that runs in your web browser and keeps your data in your own Google Drive. This policy explains what information Spendsheet uses, where it goes, and how you stay in control of it. In short: your expenses are stored only in your Google Drive, Spendsheet has no server of its own, and nothing is sold or shared.

What Spendsheet accesses in your Google Account

When you sign in with Google, Spendsheet asks for two permissions:

  • Your email address. It's shown in Spendsheet's settings so you can see which account you're using, and it's used to open your spreadsheets in that same account.
  • Google Drive files that Spendsheet creates (the drive.file permission). Spendsheet creates a folder called "Spendsheet" in your Drive and one spreadsheet per financial year inside it. It reads and writes your expenses, categories and settings in those spreadsheets. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

What data Spendsheet handles

  • Expenses you enter: amount, date, category, an optional note, and when it was added.
  • Your categories and their colours.
  • Your settings: currency and when your financial year starts.

All of this is written to the spreadsheets in your Google Drive, and nowhere else.

Where your data goes

  • Spendsheet has no server and no database. The app runs entirely in your browser, which talks directly to Google's services. Your expenses never pass through any computer run by Spendsheet.
  • Spendsheet does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or tracking.
  • Your data is not used for advertising, and is not used to develop, improve or train any AI or machine-learning models.
  • The app's files are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may record basic technical information such as your IP address when your browser downloads the app. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Signing in, Google Drive and Google Sheets are provided by Google and covered by Google's privacy policy.

What is stored in your browser

To work, Spendsheet saves a few things in your browser's local storage on your device:

  • The sign-in from Google: a temporary access token (valid for about an hour) and your email address.
  • The email you last signed in with, to make signing in again quicker.
  • Your choice of light, dark or system appearance.

Signing out removes the sign-in. You can remove all of it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Spendsheet's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google Account is used only to provide Spendsheet's features described above.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your spreadsheets stay in your Google Drive for as long as you keep them. You can edit, download or delete them at any time in Google Drive.
  • To stop Spendsheet's access, remove it in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections. Your spreadsheets are not deleted when you do this.
  • Because Spendsheet keeps no copy of your data, there is nothing on Spendsheet's side to delete.

Children

Spendsheet is not intended for children under 13.
